# Community expectations / 社区约定

欢迎不同经验和背景的贡献者。请尊重他人、围绕事实讨论，接受有依据的反馈，避免人身攻击、骚扰、歧视和公开他人的私人信息。

Welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, discuss evidence, accept constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary. Use GitHub's report controls to report abuse; never repost private or harmful content while reporting it.
